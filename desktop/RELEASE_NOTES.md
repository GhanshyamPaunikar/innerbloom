## Download Innerbloom

Pick the file for your computer from **Assets** below:

| Your computer | File |
|---|---|
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later) | `Innerbloom-mac-apple-silicon.dmg` |
| Older Mac with an Intel chip | `Innerbloom-mac-intel.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 | `Innerbloom-windows.zip` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu, then **About This Mac**, and look at **Chip**.

### Install on a Mac

1. Open the `.dmg` file and drag **Innerbloom** into **Applications**.
2. Open Innerbloom from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app, because the app isn't registered with Apple yet. Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.

### Install on Windows

1. Right-click the `.zip` file, choose **Extract All**, and extract it to a folder you'll keep (Documents is fine).
2. Open the folder and double-click **Innerbloom.exe**. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only do this once.
3. Optional: right-click Innerbloom.exe and choose **Pin to Start**.

### First start

Innerbloom walks you through two steps:

1. **Get Ollama**, the free program that runs the AI privately on your computer.
2. **Download the AI**: about 7 GB in all, one time only.

Then you can start writing. Everything stays on your computer, and nothing you write is sent anywhere.

**Works best with** 8 GB of memory or more and 10 GB of free disk space.

Your journal is saved in your own user folder, not inside the app, so updating Innerbloom never touches it:
- Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/Innerbloom`
- Windows: `%APPDATA%\Innerbloom`
