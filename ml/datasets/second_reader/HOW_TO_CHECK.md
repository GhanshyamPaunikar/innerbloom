# Hey, thanks for helping with this

I'm building a journal app called Innerbloom. When someone writes an entry, the app tries to work out how they were feeling. To check whether it's doing a good job, I wrote a load of short made-up journal entries and marked how each one feels to me.

The problem is that it's only my opinion so far. If I read things oddly, the app learns to read them oddly too. So I'd love you to go through 100 of them and tell me how *you* read them. You won't see my answers, so you can't be nudged by them. There are no wrong answers here, just your honest read.

None of these are real people's journals. I made them all up.

It takes about an hour. You don't have to do it in one go.

## What to do

Open `sheet.csv`. It opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Each row is one entry. Read it, then fill in these columns:

**Main feeling**: the one feeling that comes through most by the end. Use one word from this list:

| Feeling | What I mean by it |
|---|---|
| happy | lifted, in a good mood |
| excited | buzzing about something that's coming up |
| content | satisfied, things are fine as they are |
| calm | quiet, settled, at peace |
| grateful | thankful for something or someone |
| proud | pleased with something they did |
| hopeful | thinks things are going to get better |
| reflective | thinking things over, no strong feeling either way |
| neutral | just facts, no real feeling in it |
| tired | worn out, low on energy |
| overwhelmed | too much going on at once |
| anxious | worried, on edge, nervous |
| sad | down, low, a sense of loss |
| lonely | missing people, feeling cut off |
| frustrated | annoyed, stuck, fed up |
| angry | properly angry, usually at someone or something |

**Also fits (optional)**: if a second feeling is just as strong, put it here. Most of the time you can leave this empty.

**How strong (1-10)**: how strongly they're feeling it.
- **1-2**: barely there
- **3-4**: noticeable, but mild
- **5-6**: clearly there, it's shaping their day
- **7-8**: strong, hard to shake off
- **9-10**: it's taken over the whole entry

**Names of people**: any people named in the entry, written the way they appear (Priya, Mum, Dr. Lee). Put commas between them. Leave it empty if nobody's named. Don't include pets, places, brands or days of the week. You can leave out things like "my boss" or "my sister" unless they're used as a name ("told Mum").

**Notes (optional)**: if an entry made you stop and think, or none of the feelings fit, say so here. These notes are really useful to me.

**Code**: please don't change this one. It's how I match your answers to mine.

## A few things that come up a lot

- Go with how they feel **by the end**, not the first thing they mention.
- If they say they're *not* feeling something ("not even nervous for once"), that feeling doesn't count.
- If they're being sarcastic ("great, another 9pm meeting"), go with what they actually mean.
- If you can't decide between two, pick one for Main feeling and put the other in Also fits.
- Don't overthink it. Your first honest read is exactly what I need.

## When you're done

Save it as CSV (in Excel: File > Save As > CSV UTF-8; in Numbers: File > Export To > CSV; in Google Sheets: File > Download > CSV) and send it back to me.

Thanks so much, this genuinely makes the app better.
